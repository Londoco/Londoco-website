Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between acetone, MEK and IPA wipes?

Acetone wipes remove uncured resin, polyester and ink. MEK wipes remove cured sealant, adhesive and paint. IPA wipes clean fingerprints and light residue from electronics and optics. Heptane wipes remove petroleum oil, grease and flux and are gentler on rubber and plastics. See the full wipe range.

Do you supply pre-saturated wipes in the UK?

Yes. Londoco supplies UK-manufactured pre-saturated wipes to businesses from Bromley, England.

What are cold vulcanising conveyor belt adhesives used for?

They splice, patch and repair conveyor belts at ambient temperature, without a hot vulcanising press. Londoco is a UK distributor of Masso TRS adhesives for rubber and PVC/PU belts. See the TRS adhesive range.
