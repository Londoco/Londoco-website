Heavy Machinery & Excavation MRO

Mining operations deal with extreme dirt, heavy grease, and high-pressure hydraulic fluid contamination on excavators, drilling rigs, and conveyers.

Quarrying & Aggregates — Conveyor Belt Repair

Sand, gravel and crushed-stone operations rely on long conveyor systems that suffer constant abrasion, impact and tearing. Quick, durable belt repairs keep material moving and limit unplanned downtime. Londoco supplies Masso TRS cold vulcanising adhesives for splicing, patching and repairing rubber conveyor belts, and for lagging pulleys and drums, as well as PVC/PU adhesives and primers for plastic belting. The same systems also suit surface mineral processing and bulk material handling lines.
