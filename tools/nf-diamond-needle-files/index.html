Diamond Needle Files

Diamond Needle Files are our most popular files. Used for grinding different metals and hard materials such as tungsten carbide, steels of 40 HRC and harder, ceramic materials, glass and more — a must for any tool and die shop, extruders and repairs of hardened alloys and ceramics. Round Ø3.0mm shank, suitable for handle (handle nf or handle nf10).
