Small Diamond Discs on a Shank

Precision Diamond Discs for Contouring & Shaping.
Rotary Diamond Discs

Diamond discs are designed for the fabrication, contouring, and shaping of metals and composites, available in the most popular shapes and in 3 grit sizes. The discs are mounted on a mandrel with an adaptor for Ø3 mm Shank, and will fit any Ø 2.35 or Ø 3 mm Rotary filing or polishing machine.

Individually packed and color coded for easy identification. Discs are available with double or single side diamond plating.
