Diamond Polishers

Diamond-Impregnated Rubber Polishers for High-Lustre Finishing.
Diamond Polishers

Highest quality diamond-impregnated polishers made of long-lasting rubber, providing a uniform, consistent finish with ease of use and short working time. Designed for smoothing, polishing and achieving high-lustre finishes on metals, platinum, titanium and stainless steel. Mounted on a mandrel with an adaptor for Ø3mm shank — fits any Ø2.35mm or Ø3mm rotary filing or polishing machine.
