Sector Solution · Automotive

Automotive Manufacturing & Repair

Pre-saturated wipes for vehicle production, component assembly, detailing and workshop maintenance. View full Automotive page →

Why industrial wipes for automotive?

Automotive production and maintenance involve repeated cleaning of components, tooling, and finished surfaces. Pre-saturated wipes provide a consistent amount of cleaning fluid at the point of use and reduce open solvent containers.

Heptane lifts non-polar petroleum oils without melting rubber components or paint coats, while hydraulic wipes tackle drivetrain fluid leaks.

About Londoco

Londoco Ltd is a UK-based supplier of industrial and dental consumables, founded in 2024. We source pre-saturated solvent wipes from a UK manufacturer, and precision rotary, milling and dental burs from qualified partners, supplying customers across the UK and internationally. A bonding kit range for the mining sector is in development.

What we supply

  • Pre-saturated wipes: IPA, acetone, MEK, heptane, hydraulic, ethanol and data centre wipes, made in the UK.
  • Rotary and milling burs: tungsten carbide and diamond tools for engineering and manufacturing.
  • Dental burs and consumables: for practices and laboratories.

How we work
