Precision Consumables for Demanding Industries

Londoco Ltd is a UK-based supplier of precision-engineered technical consumables, serving industrial, engineering and dental customers across the UK and international markets.

We supply pre-saturated solvent wipes, precision rotary burs, and milling and dental burs, with a bonding kit range for the mining sector currently in development. Every product is built to the same standard: consistent performance, tight tolerances, and full traceability from batch to delivery.

Our customers span aerospace, automotive, data centres, dental, engineering & manufacturing, marine & shipbuilding, mining & extraction, and rail — sectors where surface preparation, cleanliness and component precision are not optional. Our core wipe range is manufactured in the UK to high-spec formulations (IPA, acetone, MEK and more), while our burs and dental consumables are engineered for the tolerances demanding technical and clinical work require.

As we grow beyond our current markets, our focus stays the same: reliable UK-manufactured quality, technical support customers can rely on, and a product range built for environments where failure isn't an option.

Sector Solution · Automotive

Automotive Manufacturing & Repair

Pre-saturated wipes for vehicle production, component assembly, detailing and workshop maintenance.

Why industrial wipes for automotive?

Automotive production and maintenance involve repeated cleaning of components, tooling, and finished surfaces. Pre-saturated wipes provide a consistent amount of cleaning fluid at the point of use and reduce open solvent containers.

Heptane lifts non-polar petroleum oils without melting rubber components or paint coats, while hydraulic wipes tackle drivetrain fluid leaks.
