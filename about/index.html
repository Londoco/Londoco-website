About Londoco

Londoco Ltd is a UK-based supplier of specialist technical consumables, founded in 2024. We bring four product ranges together under one supplier: pre-saturated wipes, rotary and milling burs, dental burs and consumables, and Masso TRS conveyor belt adhesives and repair systems.

Our customers work in engineering and manufacturing, aerospace, automotive, rail, marine, data centres, dental and mining. They need dependable consumables from a supplier who understands the application. We source each range from a specialist manufacturer or qualified partner, so you can get the right product for the job through one supplier and one quote request.

We supply customers across the UK and internationally.

What we supply

  • Pre-saturated wipes: IPA, acetone, MEK, heptane, hydraulic, ethanol and data centre wipes, made in the UK.
  • Rotary and milling burs: tungsten carbide and diamond tools for engineering and manufacturing.
  • Dental burs and consumables: for practices and laboratories.
  • Cold vulcanising adhesives and conveyor belt repair (Masso TRS): rubber adhesives, PVC/PU adhesives, surface primers, hardeners and cleaners, and rapid repair systems for splicing, patching, lagging and lining.
