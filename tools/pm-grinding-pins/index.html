PM Grinding Pins

Precision Mounted Grinding Pins, 0.2mm to 25mm Diameter.
Grinding Pins

PM Grinding Pins are precision mounted points used for internal grinding, deburring and fine shaping of hardened metals, carbide, and ceramics. Manufactured across an extensive diameter range with fine, medium and coarse grit options colour-coded for quick identification on the shank.
