Data Centres

Precision cleaning solutions designed for sensitive electronics and server infrastructure.

Why High-Purity IPA Wipes for Data Infrastructure?

Modern data center hardware demands zero-residue cleaning protocols to maintain optimal operating reliability. Isopropanol (IPA) pre-saturated wipes offer controlled, rapid-evaporating solvent delivery that eliminates dust, skin oils, and airborne contaminants from optical interfaces, rack frames, and sensitive contacts.

UK-Manufactured Traceability: All wipes are produced in ISO 9001 and AS9100 certified facilities ensuring chemical uniformity.

Key Applications

  • Fiber Optic Cable Connectors & Alignment Sleeves
  • Server Rack Doors, Cabinet Frames & Glass Enclosures
  • Circuit Board Pin Contacts & Diagnostic Ports
  • Cleanroom Environment Surface Degreasing
