About Londoco

Londoco Ltd is a UK-based supplier of industrial, dental and mining consumables, founded in 2024. We source pre-saturated solvent wipes from a UK manufacturer and precision rotary, milling and dental burs from qualified partners. We are also a UK distributor of Masso TRS cold vulcanising adhesives and conveyor belt repair systems. We supply customers across the UK and internationally.

What we supply

  • Pre-saturated wipes: IPA, acetone, MEK, heptane, hydraulic, ethanol and data centre wipes, made in the UK.
  • Rotary and milling burs: tungsten carbide and diamond tools for engineering and manufacturing.
  • Dental burs and consumables: for practices and laboratories.
  • Cold vulcanising adhesives and conveyor belt repair (Masso TRS): rubber adhesives, PVC/PU adhesives, surface primers, hardeners and cleaners, and rapid repair systems for splicing, patching, lagging and lining.

How we work

We vet every manufacturer before we list their products, checking relevant quality certifications (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 13485 for dental, AS9100 Rev D for aerospace) and batch traceability. Masso TRS is ISO 9001 certified, and COSHH documentation is available for the TRS range, as quarries and mining sites require. Safety and technical data sheets are available on request, and we can advise on the right product for your process.
