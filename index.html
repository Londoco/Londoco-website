Data Centres

IPA wipes supplied by Londoco for precision cleaning around live, sensitive electronic equipment.

Why IPA for data centres?

Data centres contain live, sensitive electronic equipment, making the choice of cleaning fluid particularly important. IPA wipes are widely used for technical cleaning because they evaporate quickly and leave minimal residue.

Different IPA concentrations can also be selected according to the application, with 99.9% IPA suited to precision electrical-contact and component cleaning, while 70% IPA can be used for more general cleaning and degreasing applications.

IPA is commonly selected for data-centre cleaning because it offers rapid evaporation and low residue, while more aggressive solvents may be unsuitable for certain plastics, coatings and sensitive components.

Where are IPA wipes used?

Server rack doors & frames

Removing fingerprints, dust residue and skin oils left during installation and maintenance.

Connectors, ports & fibre optic ends

Precision cleaning where contamination can affect connections and signal performance.

Keyboards, KVM switches & touchscreens

For regularly handled equipment and operator interfaces.

Raised floors & hard surfaces

For cleaning after deeper maintenance activities.

Equipment relocation & decommissioning

Cleaning equipment before relocation, resale or return to service.
