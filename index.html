Data Centres

High-performance cleaning solutions engineered specifically for data centres and critical IT infrastructure.

Product Description

Londoco Data Centre wipes is a high-performance, presaturated, ultra-low VOC cleaning wipe engineered specifically for use in data centre and critical IT environments.

Manufactured in Britain using advanced split fibre technology fabric, this wipe delivers superior contaminant removal while maintaining a controlled low saturation level to eliminate the risk of liquid ingress or equipment damage.

Designed for precision cleaning of sensitive electronic equipment, server racks, cabinets, consoles, and touch surfaces.

Performance Advantage: Independent validation confirms removal of 99.99% of surface contaminants under controlled testing conditions. The split fibre technology mechanically lifts fingerprints, dust particles, light oils, handling residues, and atmospheric contaminants.

Key Features

  • Low Saturation Formula – Minimises liquid exposure and prevents pooling or ingress into sensitive equipment
  • Anti-Static Properties – Reduces electrostatic discharge (ESD) risk during cleaning
  • Split Fibre Technology Fabric – Enhanced particle capture and superior wipe efficiency
  • Ultra-Low VOC Formulation – Suitable for enclosed environments and air-controlled data centres
  • 99.99% Contaminant Removal – High performance mechanical particle extraction
  • Residue-Free Finish – Leaves surfaces perfectly clean without film
  • Lint-Free Construction – Prevents stray fibre shedding near sensitive optics or contacts
  • Manufactured in Britain – Guaranteed quality control and batch traceability

Applications

Suitable for cleaning:

  • Server racks and cabinets & Network hardware
  • Switches, routers & Data storage units
  • Control panels, Touchscreens and monitors
  • External IT hardware surfaces, access panels & enclosures

Sector Solution · Industrial Wipes

Automotive

Pre-saturated wipes for vehicle production, component assembly, repair, detailing and workshop maintenance.

Why industrial wipes for automotive?

Automotive production and maintenance involve repeated cleaning of components, tooling and finished surfaces. Pre-saturated wipes provide a consistent amount of cleaning fluid at the point of use and reduce open solvent containers.

IPA is useful for general technical cleaning and surface preparation. Acetone, MEK and heptane provide stronger solvent action for specific residues on compatible substrates.

Consistent solvent delivery prevents overwetting and ensures repeatable surface preparation before adhesive, sealant, or coating applications.

Typical Wipe Applications

Component & part cleaning

Removing light oils, fingerprints, dust and process residues from components before assembly.

Bonding & sealing prep

Cleaning selected surfaces before adhesive, sealant, tape or coating operations.

Paint & finishing prep

Targeted removal of contamination prior to selected finishing processes.

Workshop maintenance

Cleaning tools, fixtures, work surfaces and selected mechanical components.

Interior & technical surfaces

Cleaning hard, non-porous surfaces and operator interfaces with compatible formulations.

Sector Solution · Industrial Wipes

Marine & Shipbuilding

Industrial pre-saturated wipes for shipyards, marine maintenance, vessel construction and repair.

Why industrial wipes for marine applications?

Marine construction combines large-scale fabrication with precision work around engines, hydraulics, electrical systems, coatings and composite materials. Pre-saturated wipes deliver controlled cleaning without dispensing solvent from bulk containers.

IPA is suited to light technical cleaning. Acetone, MEK and heptane address heavy residue or preparation tasks, subject to substrate compatibility.

Marine coatings and composites are solvent-sensitive; compatibility testing and manufacturer guidelines should govern solvent selection.

Shipyard & Marine Applications

Surface Preparation

Removing grease, oil, fingerprints and residues before bonding, sealing or coating.

Engine & Machinery MRO

Cleaning components, tooling and accessible hard surfaces during routine maintenance.

Hydraulic & Mechanical Work

Cleaning fittings, components and work areas prior to inspection or reassembly.
