Diamond Strip

A very thin foil plated with 100% diamond concentricity in different grit sizes. High flexibility enables a wide range of applications — can be cut with ordinary scissors to any desired shape and attached to a smooth surface with a self-adhesive back. Widely used in the die and mould industry for polishing and fine-finish work.
